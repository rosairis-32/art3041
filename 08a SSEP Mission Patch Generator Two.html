Bridging Vector Design, Mascots, & Microgravity Through Generative Code

Course: ART 3041: A.I. for Designers Author: Rosa I. Paula Term: Fall 2026

I directed Gemini to build a custom, browser-based generator—a tool to bridge vector design directly with code. At first, the drafts felt stiff and overly masculine, weighed down by a clunky interface that leaned far too hard into generic AI aesthetics. But during class, a fellow student sparked a shift, inspiring a complete visual change in the dashboard.

The motifs themselves drew life from our classroom exercises and the spirit of the Bearcat mascot. My favorite remains the design with the certified page; it carries the weight and pride of official documents. In one iteration somehow I created a version where the bearcat looks as aggressive as the actual logo. I keep this one in mind if I decide to submit to the competition.

“Ultimately, I made this patch to celebrate equal access to exploration. By launching our Bearcat into orbit, the design declares that groundbreaking discovery belongs to every curious mind, lifting Baruch’s civic roots straight into the cosmos.”

I plan to keep refining the patch. Working with AI has become an exciting part of the process, giving me a clearer, sharper way to translate how I think and communicate.
