Creative Director Pt. 1: Style Guide

Tommy Hilfiger Spring 2027 — “Modern Prep / NYC Re-Archived”
Brand: Tommy Hilfiger (Womenswear) Deliverable: 15-Piece Capsule & Style Guide Environment: NYC Launch

01 / Campaign Concept & Narrative

“NEW HERITAGE / NYC 27” reinterprets Tommy Hilfiger’s collegiate East Coast vocabulary through an elevated, effortless feminine lens designed specifically for New York City living.

Synthesizing the sun-bleached coastal ease of the Spring 2006 Women’s Runway with the bold, graphic momentum of the Spring 2027 Runway Campaign, this wardrobe pairs tailored trousers, breezy unbuttoned shirting, flowing tiered poplin skirts, and fine-gauge knitwear for transit speed and street elegance.

05 / Logomark Concept

TOMMY HILFIGER · NYC 27

Concept: “TH ’27 / THE REGATTA SHIELD”
A delicate monoline crest that encloses the iconic red-and-white split flag within an arched heritage cartouche. Refined for soft-touch embossed hangtags, gold engraved buttons, slingback footbeds, and polished editorial ads.
